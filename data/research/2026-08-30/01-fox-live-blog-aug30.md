## Iran attacks US forces in Jordan after American strikes on missile launchers (pinned, breaking)

Iran began attacking U.S. forces in Jordan on Sunday after American forces struck Iranian missile launchers on Larak Island, a U.S. source told Fox News' Trey Yingst. ## Trump warns US or Europe would be 'next' if Iran gets nuclear weapon

President Trump said Sunday that Iran would have used a nuclear weapon if it had one, warning that Israel and much of the Middle East could have been destroyed, with the United States or Europe possibly "next." "If they would have had a nuclear weapon, they would have fired it," Trump said on "Sunday Night in America." "These are very violent, evil people." "So I think the Middle East would have essentially been gone, but Israel definitely would have been gone… I guess we would be next, or Europe, or someplace. But no, you cannot let them have a nuclear weapon, and they won't have a nuclear weapon." Trump also praised the blockade and financial pressure: Iran had become "a failing nation." "The blockade has been unbelievable. The financial hits that we're giving them… Their navy's gone. Their air force is gone. Their leaders are gone. All of their anti-aircraft — I mean, it's all wiped out. They're tough people." He said he was surprised when Tehran began targeting Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UAE, Bahrain and Kuwait at the start of the war, but the attacks eroded Iran's regional support: "Everybody was shocked. I was surprised. I thought it was a real good thing because they lost all the support that they had."

## US to hit Iran with new sanctions every week as Bessent puts global banks on notice

The U.S. Treasury plans to roll out new sanctions targeting Iran on a weekly basis, beginning with banks that help Tehran access the global financial system, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Reuters (https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/bessent-expects-new-us-secondary-sanctions-weekly-aiming-increase-pressure-iran-2026-08-30/) on Sunday. "You're going to see a lot more of these every week," Bessent said ahead of a G20 finance leaders meeting in North Carolina. "We're starting with the banks, and we're telling the banks it's not okay to have Iranian money and to aid the regime." Bessent said the administration could go as far as cutting a financial institution off entirely from the U.S. dollar-based financial system. His comments came after Treasury imposed penalties Friday on the UAE branches of Egypt's Banque Misr.

## US struck Iranian launchers after intel uncovered plot to fire mine-carrying rockets into Hormuz

U.S. forces struck Iranian missile launchers on Larak Island on Sunday after intelligence indicated Tehran was preparing to fire rockets carrying sea mines into the Strait of Hormuz, Trey Yingst reported — the plan designed to redeploy explosives into the strait after U.S. forces demined it (on "The Big Weekend Show"; https://www.foxnews.com/video/6404335766112). Trump had said Aug. 25 the Navy informed him all mines had been removed, and warned any vessel laying new ones would be "immediately and systematically destroyed." U.S. forces in Jordan faced missile attacks Sunday in retaliation; Yingst: American forces in Jordan have multiple air-defense systems including Patriot and THAAD. "At this point, there are no significant impacts, and nearly all of the incoming missiles have been intercepted. These are serious attacks."

## Jordan intercepts 8 Iranian missiles after US forces strike launchers

## IRGC claims combined missile, drone attacks on two US air bases in Jordan
