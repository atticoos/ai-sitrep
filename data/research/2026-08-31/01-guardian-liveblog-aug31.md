# Trump says US will 'hit Iran hard' as hostilities resume – as it happened

Iran responded to an assault on its Larak Island by targeting US bases in Jordan

First published Mon 31 Aug 2026 04.01 EDT, updated 13.17 EDT. [Key events]

- **The US president, Donald Trump, has told Fox News's chief foreign correspondent, Trey Yingst, that the US will respond to Iran targeting US military bases in Jordan overnight.** "We're going to hit them hard," Trump told Fox News. "There will be a response," he added, after Jordan's army confirmed it had intercepted eight missiles early this morning.

- **Trump was sending a message to Iran with his social media post about Kharg Island, vice-president JD Vance said on Monday.** "The president likes to switch it up on social media a little bit. He would be the first to say he doesn't make announcements about military actions on social media, but I think that he's sending a message to the Iranians," Vance said when asked about Trump's post.

- **Trump's one-line social media post on Sunday was accompanied by an AI-generated video clip showing fake attacks by the US military.** A US official said on Monday the military had not targeted Kharg Island in overnight strikes. An Iranian official dismissed Trump's post as "laughable".

- **US treasury secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC in an interview on Monday that the goal of US sanctions on Iran is "to create the conditions that they will want to come to the table" for negotiations.** As Bessent opened meetings of the Group of 20 finance ministers in Asheville on Monday, he said he wants to focus on ways to grow the global economy and rein in mounting debt.

- **Bessent also briefly touched on the new Iran sanctions earlier this morning, before his opening remarks in Asheville.** Speaking to reporters, Bessent predicted that, under the latest round of sanctions, Iran's economy could collapse within "weeks or months". But he also suggested that a deal, not collapse, was the administration's primary goal. "The economy doesn't have to collapse, we just have to have the regime come to their senses," Bessent said.

- **In a brief post to his Truth Social platform, Trump claimed Iran is a "failed nation" with no navy, air force or currency.** "IT IS DEAD!" he wrote, saying the only things Tehran have in its favour are a sympathetic US media, a "willingness to kill their protesters" and a "good line of bullshit". Trump said the Iranian leadership is in "total disarray" and is "incapable" of properly representing the country.

- **The UAE condemned an Iranian drone attack intercepted over its territorial waters, calling it a "dangerous escalation".** In a statement on X, the UAE's foreign ministry "strongly condemned the hostile Iranian attack on the country involving a drone that was intercepted by air defence systems over UAE territorial waters".

- **US Central Command (Centcom) has denied claims made by Iran state TV and the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps that a ship had struck mines in the strait of Hormuz.** In a statement posted to social media, Centcom said "Iran's claim that a supertanker passing through strait of Hormuz struck two mines and was brought to a complete stop is false."

- **The EU issued a statement saying it will work with the US and other allied countries to maintain economic pressure against Iran.** "The EU welcomes efforts at ensuring that Iran ceases its destabilising activities and engages in peace negotiations with good faith, also through additional economic pressure, including through the US-led Operation Economic Outcast."

- Israel signed a $3.5bn defence export deal with Greece on Monday ("Achilles Shield", incl. a multilayered air defence system); Lebanon's NNA reported Israeli shelling in southern Lebanon; Gaza toll updates (out of campaign scope, logged only).

## Exchange detail

In response to the US assault on Larak Island late on Sunday, the IRGC "targeted technical infrastructure, maintenance facilities and enemy fighter jet positions at the US-operated King Hussein and al-Azraq airbases in Jordan" with ballistic missiles, causing "heavy damage", according to state TV. In the interview with Fox News on Monday, Trump said all of the missiles were intercepted by American air defence systems, except one which was allowed to pass through the airspace because it was identified as not on course to hit anything "significant", according to Yingst.

## Supertanker-mines exchange

Reuters snap: CENTCOM denied claims made by Iran state TV and the IRGC that a ship had struck mines in the strait of Hormuz. "Iran's claim that a supertanker passing through strait of Hormuz struck two mines and was brought to a complete stop is false." The denial followed reports in state media earlier today that Iran had said an oil tanker caught fire in the strait after it hit two naval mines. "A rogue supertanker attempting to pass through the illegal route south of the strait of Hormuz caught fire in massive fires after striking two naval mines and was brought to a complete stop," Iran's Revolutionary Guards said in an earlier statement.

## Bessent / G20 Asheville

Bessent told CNBC on Monday the goal of sanctions is "to create the conditions that they will want to come to the table." Bessent told AP in an interview on Sunday that he plans to turn up pressure on Iran by sanctioning another bank, part of "Operation Economic Outcast," and said the administration is prepared to exact "financial violence if we have to." Reuters: Bessent argued Iran is resorting to kinetic military responses only because it is losing economically, and that Iran's economy does not need to collapse — the Iranian government needs to "come to its senses."

## Iran's economy (Patrick Wintour)

For many Iranians, Operation Economic Outcast seems superfluous given the grim economic situation: vegetable oil in Iran in August cost 383% more than a year ago; eggs +294%, chicken +177%, red meat +148%. Mehr: demand for red meat fell 50% in April 2026 y/y; leap in potato consumption leading to shortages. "They don't understand that people can no longer afford even a normal life," Golshan Fathi, an Iranian feminist, said. ## Pezeshkian at SCO Bishkek / Modi meeting

Statement from the Iranian presidency on the Modi meeting: "We continue to strive for a path of agreement and understanding, and we believe that continuing the war is neither in our interest, nor in the interest of the region. We believe that the solution to the existing problems is dialogue and cooperation, and all capacities must be used to prevent the spread of insecurity and conflict."
