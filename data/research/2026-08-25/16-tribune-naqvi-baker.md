US chargé d’affaires praises CDF Munir’s diplomatic role for regional peace, stability

In a meeting with interior minister, Natalie Baker exchanged views on enhancing Pak-US cooperation in various fields

United States Chargé d’Affaires Natalie Baker on Tuesday praised Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir for promoting regional peace and stability during a meeting with Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi.

According to a statement issued by the Ministry of Interior, Natalie called on Naqvi in Islamabad, during which the two sides discussed the overall regional situation, peace and stability.

“The US ambassador appreciated CDF Munir’s positive diplomatic role in promoting peace and stability in the region,” it said.

The ministry further said the two sides exchanged views on enhancing Pakistan-US cooperation in various fields, including programmes and training opportunities for police officers.

“The meeting also discussed measures to expand cooperation against organised crime, drug trafficking and human smuggling,” it added.

Secretary for the Ministry of Interior was also present at the meeting.

Naqvi says 'significant progress' made in talks with Iranian president to end conflict

Separately, Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi said on Tuesday that “significant progress” had been made in talks with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian to end the conflict in the region during his recent visit to Tehran alongside Chief of Defence Forces (CDF) and Chief of Army Staff, Field Marshal Asim Munir.

“Significant progress was made, and the meeting concluded on a highly positive note. We remain hopeful that this momentum will help pave the way for further progress and lasting peace in the region,” Naqvi said in a post on X.

their day-long visit to Tehran on Monday, where they pushed for breaking the stalemate in the ongoing US-Iran conflict, reopening the Strait of Hormuz and finding a negotiated settlement to end the conflict and wider regional instability.

During their meeting with Pezeshkian, Naqvi said he and CDF Munir had a “very positive and productive” discussion with the Iranian president.

“Our discussion focused on the US-Iran conflict, tensions in the Middle East and the way forward, as well as the steps needed by both sides for restoration of the Islamabad MoU thereby leading to comprehensive settlement of the conflict,” he said.

Naqvi further said the Iranian president had candidly shared his government’s perspective and concerns, adding that they had a “very constructive exchange” on the issues involved.
