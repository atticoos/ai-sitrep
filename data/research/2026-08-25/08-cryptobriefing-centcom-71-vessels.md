The U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) has taken significant action in the ongoing maritime confrontation with Iran, leading to the diversion of 71 vessels, while disabling three and inspecting two others. The operation continues to allow over 40 humanitarian aid vessels to pass through the transit corridor. This development is part of a broader U.S. naval blockade aiming to curb Iranian maritime activities in the Arabian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, and Arabian Sea. The increased level of enforcement, including vessel disabling and boarding, points to heightened tensions and military engagement in the region.
