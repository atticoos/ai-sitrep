Abbas Araghchi hosts Albusaidi amid ongoing consultations over Strait of Hormuz

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met with his Omani counterpart Badr Albusaidi in Tehran on Tuesday for talks on bilateral and regional issues.

Albusaidi arrived in the Iranian capital as part of ongoing political consultations between the two countries, according to Iran's state-run IRNA news agency.

Ahead of the visit, Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei said the talks would address bilateral relations and regional issues. Baqaei said the visit was aimed at strengthening Iran-Oman cooperation and continuing sustained political consultations between the two countries as littoral states of the Strait of Hormuz. He added that the consultations were intended to contribute to peace and stability in the region.

The visit comes amid ongoing consultations between Tehran and Muscat over arrangements concerning the Strait of Hormuz, a critical route for global oil and gas exports.
