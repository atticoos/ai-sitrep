Aug 26, 06:08 PM IST — IRAN SETS CONDITIONS FOR REOPENING STRAIT OF HORMUZ:
Iran will not reopen the Strait of Hormuz unless the United States accepts Tehran's conditions and returns to an agreement reached with Oman, an IRGC spokesperson said Wednesday. The spokesperson said Iran and Oman had held talks over the past month and reached understandings on waterway access and revenue sharing. The strait could reopen if Washington stopped obstructing the agreement, but would remain closed if the US rejected Iran's terms. He also claimed Iran controlled passage through the waterway and said hostile warships were at least 400 km (250 miles) away.

Aug 26, 04:42 PM IST — PEZESHKIAN REJECTS US ECONOMIC PRESSURE, BACKS NEGOTIATIONS:
President Masoud Pezeshkian said Wednesday Tehran would continue pursuing disputes through engagement and negotiations while firmly resisting US economic pressure. Measures prepared by Iran's economic officials would prevent Washington from achieving its goals. He defended supporters of the Tehran–Washington memorandum of understanding, rejecting claims they had capitulated.

Aug 26, 10:27 PM IST — TRUMP SAYS IRAN'S KHAMENEI 'VERY SERIOUSLY WOUNDED' BUT NOT DEAD:
Trump told conservative host Glenn Beck he believes Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei is "very seriously wounded" but not dead; if dead, Iranian officials were doing a convincing job of concealing it — they continue to refer to "going back to talk to him" for final approval.

Aug 26, 10:02 PM IST — IRAN-OMAN HORMUZ PLAN: INBOUND SHIPS TO USE IRAN WATERS; PERMANENT ROUTE DISCUSSED IN 30–60 DAYS:
Commercial ships entering the Persian Gulf from the Gulf of Oman would travel entirely through Iranian waters, according to Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi. Ships leaving would use a route passing through both Iranian and Omani territorial waters. The route would initially operate as a temporary framework; a long-term arrangement would be discussed within 30 to 60 days. The emerging deal faces opposition from the Trump administration, which has objected to parts of the arrangement.

Aug 26, 09:29 PM IST — IRAN TO BAR MILITARY SHIPS FROM HORMUZ UNDER OMAN DEAL:
Iran says military ships will not be allowed to transit the Strait of Hormuz under its proposed agreement with Oman, deputy foreign minister said.

Aug 26, 07:57 PM IST — WHAT IRAN WANTS FROM WASHINGTON:
Reopening depends on US compliance with the June memorandum: lift sanctions; end the naval blockade of Iranian ports; unfreeze Iranian assets held overseas; return to the earlier agreement. Washington has not indicated readiness to accept these conditions.

Aug 26, 07:39 PM IST — US ACCUSED OF DELAYING HORMUZ DEAL:
IRGC spokesperson Hossein Mohebi said US interference had delayed finalising the arrangement. The dispute particularly focuses on Tehran's demand that ships pay for passage — a proposal Washington has rejected. Iran began charging some vessels transit fees early in the conflict, later describing them as fees for services. Oman has opposed formal transit fees, though it has reportedly indicated vessels could pay for related services.

Aug 26, 06:48 PM IST — TRUMP 'NOT IN A HURRY' OVER IRAN TALKS:
"I have no time schedule; I'm not in a hurry," Trump said when asked about negotiations, maintaining both economic and military pressure were proving effective.

Aug 26, 05:14 PM IST — IRGC CALLS MEDIA 'FRONT LINE' IN PERCEPTION WAR:
IRGC spokesperson Hossein Mohebi said modern conflicts are fought over perception; urged domestic media to promote hope and national resolve; said the conflict is increasingly technology-driven (AI, software, drone data).

Aug 26, 04:35 PM IST — IRAN ASKS QATAR FOR ANSWERS ON 3 MISSING PILOTS:
Iran urged Qatar to do more to determine the fate of three Iranian Su-24 pilots missing since their aircraft were shot down in March. Army spokesperson Mohammad Akraminia said Doha had told Tehran it was unaware of their fate; Iran wants a "clear and documented response." Qatar has denied earlier Iranian claims the pilots were captured alive, saying it recovered the remains of one pilot after two Iranian Su-24s entered Qatari airspace and were shot down.

Aug 26, 02:35 PM IST — PEZESHKIAN, PUTIN TO MEET AT SCO SUMMIT:

Aug 26, 02:29 PM IST — EXPLOSIONS EXPECTED NEAR TEHRAN AS IRAN DISPOSES OF UNEXPLoded MUNITIONS:
Explosions may be heard in Pakdasht, southeast of Tehran, on Wednesday as authorities dispose of unexploded munitions left from attacks; sounds could continue until 4 p.m. local time (IRGC Tehran province).
