CNBC TV18 live blog, Aug 26. Entries with Aug 26 IST timestamps (IST = UTC+5:30). Trimmed of navigation and non-Iran regional items noted only briefly.

Aug 26, 9:42 PM IST — IRANIAN WARSHIP CONFRONTS INDIAN TANKER ATTEMPTING HORMUZ TRANSIT: FARS:
Iran's Fars news agency said a large Iranian warship confronted an Indian oil tanker attempting to pass through the southern route of the Strait of Hormuz, known as the 'Oman corridor'. The tanker, identified as HAANA, was reportedly deterred after receiving a warning, with Fars saying no vessels had used the route in the previous 24 hours.

Aug 26, 3:05 PM IST — QATAR'S LNG EXPORTS PLUNGE 96%:
Six months into the war, Qatar has emerged as one of its biggest economic casualties, with LNG exports falling 96% (Reuters). The country has lost an estimated $24 billion in gas sales — equal to about five months of income based on 2025 figures. Oil exports from Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Iraq and Kuwait have also been hit, but far less severely.

Aug 26, 2:44 PM IST — IRANIAN STRIKES CAUSED BILLIONS IN DAMAGE TO US SPY SITES: REPORT:
Iranian missile and drone attacks have caused unprecedented damage to US intelligence facilities and surveillance systems across West Asia, NBC News reported, citing four people familiar with the matter. Strikes damaged buildings and intelligence-gathering equipment operated by the CIA and other agencies; repairs expected to cost billions; destruction described as more extensive than any previously suffered by American intelligence operations. [See 06-yahoo-dailybeast file for the Daily Beast's detailed recap of the same NBC report.]

Aug 26, 10:40 AM IST — VIDEO AIRED ON IRAN STATE TV THREATENS TRUMP'S YOUNGEST SON; SECRET SERVICE INVESTIGATING:
A video aired on Iranian state television threatened Donald Trump's youngest son, Barron Trump; the Secret Service is aware (Reuters). The three-minute broadcast claimed the 20-year-old was being monitored and that a $10 million bounty had been offered for his killing.

Aug 26, 12:58 PM IST — US SUPPORT FOR IRAN WAR FALLS TO 31% AS TRUMP'S APPROVAL REMAINS AT RECORD LOW:
Public support for US military action against Iran has fallen to 31%, its lowest since the conflict's early days (Reuters/Ipsos). Backing among Republicans dropped to 69% from 77% in March. Trump's job approval remained at 33% for a second consecutive survey, the lowest recorded by Reuters/Ipsos across either of his terms.

Aug 26, 12:13 PM IST — CHINA EMERGES AS A MAJOR TEST FOR TRUMP'S IRAN SANCTIONS CAMPAIGN:
The sanctions campaign may be constrained by the effort to preserve a trade truce with China ahead of President Xi Jinping's expected Washington visit next month. "The announcement yesterday was very careful in my view to avoid specifics (against China), which could have led to a disruption in the summit," Edgard Kagan of CSIS told AP.

Aug 26, 11:38 AM IST — BESSENT DISCUSSES IRAN SANCTIONS CAMPAIGN WITH BAHRAIN FINANCE MINISTER:
Treasury readout (via @USTreasury, August 26): Bessent spoke with Bahrain's Finance Minister Shaikh Salman bin Khalifa Al Khalifa about "Operation Economic Outcast"; called for sustained pressure on Iran's remaining economic lifelines; said Tehran must not be allowed to use the Strait of Hormuz as leverage against the global economy; both sides agreed to coordinate with GCC partners to combat illicit finance.

Aug 26, 12:23 PM IST — IMF CHIEF: GLOBAL ECONOMY WEATHERED HORMUZ OIL SHOCK 'BETTER THAN FEARED':
Kristalina Georgieva credited withdrawals from reserves, higher non-Gulf supplies and an AI investment boom. "The energy shock is not over"; another rise in oil prices could push up inflation and keep rates elevated. Outlook described as a tug-of-war between the West Asia supply shock and AI-driven demand.

Aug 26, 1:42 PM IST — IRAN URGES UN MEMBERS TO REJECT US ECONOMIC PRESSURE CAMPAIGN:
Iran's deputy permanent representative to the UN, Gholamhossein Darzi, told the Security Council Washington's campaign violated countries' sovereign rights and blamed the US-Israeli war for disrupting shipping through Hormuz (IRNA): "We call on all governments to reject and condemn the US regime's use of unilateral coercive measures outside its borders…"

Aug 26, 1:17 PM IST — USS ABRAHAM LINCOLN HEADS TO THAILAND AFTER RECORD DEPLOYMENT AT SEA:
The carrier will make a brief Thailand stop for crew rest after 250+ consecutive days at sea supporting the war; Thailand's navy said three ships from the strike group would visit, no exercises planned.

Aug 26, 7:48 PM IST — IRAN WARNS HOSTILE MEDIA OUTLETS COULD BE TREATED AS MILITARY TARGETS:
Armed forces spokesperson Brig.-Gen. Abolfazl Shekarchi accused BBC, Iran International and Radio Farda of links to Israeli and US intelligence agencies; said people working for them could be included on a list of potential targets.

Aug 26, 7:16 PM IST — JAPAN PLANS TO DIVERSIFY FUEL SUPPLIES AMID HORMUZ CLOSURE:
After 93% of Japan's crude imports passed through the strait in 2025, the government plans pipeline bypass projects with Middle Eastern producers; specific measures expected by year-end.

Aug 26, 9:08 AM IST — TRUMP SAYS ALL MINES CLEARED FROM STRAIT OF HORMUZ:
Trump said all mines had been detonated or removed from international waters; any vessel laying new mines would be destroyed; the US Space Force was watching "every square inch" of the waterway.

Aug 26, 11:09 AM IST — IRAN SAYS HORMUZ REMAINS CLOSED DESPITE CORRIDOR TALKS:
Tehran said the waterway will not reopen until the US ends the war, lifts its blockade and fulfils its commitments.

Aug 26, 10:19 AM IST — TRUMP SENDS US-SAUDI NUCLEAR PACT TO CONGRESS:
Classified agreement supporting Saudi Arabia's civilian nuclear programme submitted for review; signed last month by the two energy ministers; Trump said it will not proceed unless Riyadh normalises with Israel under the Abraham Accords.

Aug 26, 9:45 AM IST — IRANIAN EDITOR SAYS US MAY DELAY WIDER STRIKES UNTIL AFTER MIDTERMS:
Mokhtar Haddad, editor-in-chief of Iranian newspaper Al-Wefaq, told Al Jazeera recent US remarks ruling out new strikes could signal an effort to manage the standoff until the midterms: "I do not think Iran will wait while America merely seeks to get past the election period and then return to broader fighting." He dismissed the latest measures: "They are the same sanctions."

Iran will continue to stand firm; Tehran remains open to negotiations.

Aug 26, 3:46 PM IST — DUBAI AIRPORT PASSENGER TRAFFIC PLUNGES 31% IN H1 2026:
31.5 million passengers vs 46 million a year earlier, as war disrupts Middle East air travel.

Aug 26, 2:15 PM IST — HILLARY CLINTON SAYS US STRATEGY UNDERESTIMATED IRAN'S STAYING POWER:
"The misconception on a strategic level has led us to where we are"; killing senior leaders was never likely to make the IRGC abandon the fight.
